03 / Brand

The mark, the lockup, and how to use them.

The Pac Technologies mark is a stylised globe formed from a counter-rotating arc and three orbital bands — a control loop, suggested. The wordmark sits to its right with no divider between them; the two read as one object. It is the only graphic device that should ever be applied to the brand.

Don't

Six ways to break the brand. Don't.

Don't recolour the mark
Pac Blue 600 is the only colour the mark may take, except white on Pac Blue 700.
Don't stretch or skew
Scale uniformly. The bands are drawn to a specific aspect ratio.
Don't place on signal colours
Red, amber, and green carry operational meaning. The brand never sits on them.
Don't enclose the mark
No circles, badges, or seals around it. The mark is its own container.
Don't tilt or animate
The lockup is read horizontally. Movement is reserved for the system around it.
Don't add gradients
No gradient backgrounds behind the mark. The system is flat by intent.
